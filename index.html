FAQ

Before you book.

How far does Batwana travel?

Our standard planning radius is up to 40 km from Nairobi. Events farther away can still be considered, with travel quoted separately before booking.

How early should I book?

Lead time depends on event size. Smaller events can usually be planned sooner; larger funpark-style events need more setup and coordination. Send the date and headcount first and we’ll confirm what is realistic.

Do you provide crew?

Yes. Packages include Batwana crew for setup, equipment operation and activity supervision. Exact staffing depends on the equipment and headcount.

Can you work with schools and companies?

Yes. We support school fun days, corporate family days, estates, community events and other organised family occasions.

How do I confirm a booking?

Start by checking your date. We confirm scope, location, final price, travel and payment terms in writing before any payment is requested.
